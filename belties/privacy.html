Concierge privacy

How Belle, the digital concierge, treats what you share, in plain language.

About this preview

This is a private preview built by Molini Studio. In this demo, any details you share in a chat or on a call go only to Molini Studio, not to Belties’ staff, and are used only to show how the concierge works.

What it asks for

Only what it needs. It answers questions without asking who you are. If you’d like the team to follow up, it asks for your name and a way to reach you, and optionally a date and the size of your group.

On the phone

When you call (434) 313-8183, Belle says at the start that the call may be recorded. A written transcript of each call is kept so we can keep answers accurate, and your caller ID is shared with Belle so she can offer to use it as your callback number. Belle’s voice is created by a speech provider from her written replies. If you ask for a person, your call is connected to Molini Studio during this demo.

What it never asks for

Payment details, card numbers, ID numbers or passwords. If you’re ever asked for these in a chat or on a call, please stop and contact Belties directly at 434-218-0352.

Who sees it

Follow-up requests go by email to the Belties team so they can reply personally. Molini Studio, which maintains the concierge, can also see conversations to keep answers accurate.

How it’s used

Conversations help us improve the concierge’s answers. They are never sold or shared for marketing.

A person is always close by

Belle is a digital helper, and our team personally confirms anything important. To reach someone, ask Belle to pass along a note, or contact Belties at 434-218-0352 or scott@belties.com.

Your choices

You can ask what’s been saved from your conversation, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing brad@molinistudio.com.
