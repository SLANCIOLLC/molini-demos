Concierge privacy

How the digital concierge treats what you share, online and on the phone, in plain language.

About this preview

This is a private preview built by Molini Studio. In this demo, any details you share in a chat or a call go only to Molini Studio, not to Dancing Bear’s staff, and are used only to show how the concierge works. Asking for a person on the demo line rings Molini Studio.

What it asks for

Only what it needs. It answers questions without asking who you are. If you’d like the team to follow up, it asks for your name and email, and optionally a phone number, dates and party size. On a call, it asks for your name and the best number to reach you.

On the phone

The concierge tells you when it answers that the call may be recorded. Calls are transcribed so the team has your request exactly as you said it, and the number you call from is logged. To speak with a person, just ask, and the concierge connects you.

What it never asks for

Payment details, card numbers, ID numbers or passwords. If you’re ever asked for these in a chat or on a call, please call the lodge instead at (865) 448-6000.

Who sees it

Follow-up requests go by email to the Dancing Bear team so they can reply personally. Molini Studio, which maintains the concierge, can also see conversations and call transcripts to keep answers accurate.

How it’s used

Conversations help us improve the concierge’s answers. They are never sold or shared for marketing, and the AI companies behind the concierge don’t use them to train their models.

A person is always close by

The concierge is a digital helper, and our team personally confirms reservations and anything important. To talk with someone, call the lodge at (865) 448-6000 or Appalachian Bistro at (865) 336-2138.

Your choices

You can ask what’s been saved from your conversation, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing brad@molinistudio.com.

This notice follows Molini Studio’s AI ethics and privacy standards and works alongside Dancing Bear Lodge’s privacy policy. Last updated November 2, 2026.
