Concierge privacy

How Cathalan, the digital concierge, treats what you share, online and on the phone, in plain language.

About this preview

This is a private preview built by Molini Studio. In this demo, any details you share in a chat or a call go only to Molini Studio, not to Jefferson Vineyards’ staff, and are used only to show how the concierge works. Asking for a person on the demo line rings Molini Studio.

What it asks for

Only what it needs. It answers questions without asking who you are. If you’d like the team to follow up, it asks for your name and email, and optionally a phone number, a date and the size of your group. On a call, it asks for your name and the best number to reach you.

On the phone

Cathalan tells you when it answers that the call may be recorded. Calls are transcribed so the team has your request exactly as you said it, and the number you call from is logged. To speak with a person during Tasting Room hours, just ask, and Cathalan connects you.

What it never asks for

Payment details, card numbers, ID numbers or passwords. If you’re ever asked for these in a chat or on a call, please call Jefferson Vineyards instead at 434-977-3042.

Who sees it

Follow-up requests go by email to the Jefferson Vineyards team so they can reply personally. Molini Studio, which maintains the concierge, can also see conversations and call transcripts to keep answers accurate.

How it’s used

Conversations help us improve the concierge’s answers. They are never sold or shared for marketing.

A person is always close by

Cathalan is a digital helper, and our team personally confirms anything important. To talk with someone, call Jefferson Vineyards at 434-977-3042.

Your choices

You can ask what’s been saved from your conversation, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing brad@molinistudio.com.

This notice follows Molini Studio’s AI ethics and privacy standards and works alongside Jefferson Vineyards’ privacy policy. Last updated November 4, 2026.
