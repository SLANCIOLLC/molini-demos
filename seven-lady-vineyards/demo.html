What Vera will never do

  • Promise a date, a table or a tasting

    The concierge gathers the date, party size and occasion, and your team confirms.

  • Quote a price or policy you haven’t published

    If it isn’t on your website or approved by your team, the question goes to your team.

  • Handle personal matters alone

    Allergies, complaints, events and anything delicate go to the right person, with the details already gathered.

Why Molini

Virginia wine runs in my family.

My relative, Antonio Molini, came to Virginia in 1773 with Philip Mazzei to plant vines at Colle, two miles from Monticello. Jefferson names him in a letter. I named my studio for him.

I worked in a Charlottesville tasting room and saw how much of a great team’s day goes to answering the same questions. I want Virginia’s wine estates to have the same tools the largest companies have, so the people you already employ have more time for the guests in front of them.

Brad O’Brien, founder, Molini Studio
